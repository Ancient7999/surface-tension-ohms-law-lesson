# Surface tension and Ohm's law

An interactive lesson covering two physics practicals:

- **Lab 8: Surface tension by capillary rise.** A capillary tube is rinsed and dipped in a liquid. The height of the column is read through a magnifier on a centimetre scale. Three readings give the average height, and the surface tension follows from γ = h r ρ g / (2 cos θ).
- **Lab 9: Ohm's law.** A resistor is wired to a DC supply with an ammeter and a voltmeter. Readings are taken from 2 V to 10 V and plotted as an I–V graph. The resistance is found from R = V/I and from the slope, R = 1/S.

Each lab has six pages:

1. Practical: an interactive bench with a guided tutorial.
2. Aim and outcomes.
3. Theory.
4. Method.
5. Observation and calculation, with worked example values.
6. Multiple-choice questions.

In the calculation panels, every symbol and number can be hovered or tapped to show its meaning and where it comes from.

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step. Open `index.html` or serve the folder with any static file server.

Fonts: Manrope and Inter (SIL Open Font License). Light and dark themes follow the system setting; the button at the top right switches between them.
